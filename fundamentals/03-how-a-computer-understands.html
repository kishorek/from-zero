How does a computer understand instructions?

One idea. The computer follows spelling rules. Quotes change what the plus sign does.

Start with a normal day

A clerk follows the form in front of them. They do not guess what you meant. They follow the marks on the form.

Your file is the form. A helper program reads it and carries out the steps. If a mark is wrong, the helper stops and points at the line.

The same plus sign can add, or it can glue words together. The quotes decide which rule applies.

New words

These words are enough for the two lines below.

helper
The program that reads your file and carries out the steps. People also call it an engine. Inside this course’s tools, that helper’s product name is V8. You can forget the product name.
number
A value used for arithmetic. Digits with no quotes, such as 2.
text
Words or digits inside quotes, such as "2".
quotes
The marks " that turn digits into text.
+
On numbers, this sign adds. On text, this sign glues the pieces into one longer text.
console.log
The spelling for “show this on the screen.”

The steps, before any program

  1. Add the numbers 2 and 3. Show the result.
  2. Take the text “2” and the text “3”. Glue them. Show the result.

Both steps use a plus sign. The quotes are the only difference.

The same steps, written as a program

Read the two steps again. The first line has no quotes, so it adds. The second line has quotes, so it glues. Predict both results, then look.

What you should see
5
23

What each line is saying

  1. console.log(2 + 3);Show the sum of two numbers. There are no quotes, so 2 and 3 are numbers. 2 plus 3 is 5.
  2. console.log("2" + "3");Show two pieces of text glued together. The quotes make text. The screen shows 23.

Try it

The line glues the text “2” to the number 3. Press Run. You should see 23.

Practice. One side is text. Guess the result, then press Run.

Easy to mix up

  • The helper follows the marks you typed. It does not guess your goal.
  • A plus sign adds numbers. A plus sign glues text.
  • When a message appears, read the line number first. The helper is pointing at a line.

Remember

A helper reads your file and follows spelling rules. Quotes turn digits into text, and then the plus sign glues instead of adding.
