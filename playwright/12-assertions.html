Assertions

One idea. A check on a locator keeps looking. A check on a copied string looks once.

The idea

An assertion says what should be true. await expect(locator).toBeVisible(), toHaveText, toHaveValue, toBeEnabled, and toBeChecked are web-first assertions. They retry until the timeout.

await expect(page).toHaveTitle and toHaveURL retry as well. The moment you pull a plain value out, retrying stops. const text = await locator.textContent(); expect(text).toBe("Saved") checks once. If the text arrives a moment later, the test fails even though a person would have seen it.

Why it matters

This single distinction explains a huge share of “it fails, but when I look, the text is there.” You looked after the check. The locator assertion would have looked again.

A picture to hold

Two inspectors. One stays at the platform and watches the board until the train name appears. The other glances once and leaves. Web-first expect stays. expect on an already-fetched string glances once.

A small example

Line by line

  1. <p data-testid="status">Waiting</p>A test id is a dedicated attribute, data-testid, that exists for automation. It does not depend on styling.
  2. textContent = "Submitted"The page changes its own text shortly after load. A one-shot check can lose this race.
  3. await expect(page.getByTestId("status")).toHaveText("Submitted");Retry until the text is exactly Submitted. Exact means the whole string. toContainText is the looser cousin when you mean “includes.”

Try it yourself

Reload, then check once immediately. Then keep looking. The second way sees Submitted.

Common mistakes

  • Using toHaveText when the element has extra whitespace you do not care about. toHaveText can take a pattern. Or assert the part that matters with toContainText.
  • Asserting a CSS class to mean “the success message showed.” Assert the text a person reads.
  • Dropping await in front of expect(...).toHaveText. You get a floating promise and a false sense of success.

Recap

Expect on a locator retries. Expect on a string you already pulled does not. Prefer toHaveText, toBeVisible, toHaveURL, and toHaveTitle.
