Actions

One idea. click presses. fill replaces the whole field.

The idea

An action is a locator plus a verb. click presses. fill clears an input and types the whole value at once, which is what you want for form fields. press sends one key, such as Enter. check and uncheck handle checkboxes. selectOption chooses from a <select>.

Playwright refuses to act on an element that is hidden, disabled, or still moving. That refusal is protection. The auto-waiting lesson explains the rules. This lesson is the verbs.

Why it matters

Most bugs in hand-written scripts come from typing into the wrong kind of element, or from sending keys one by one when fill was the right tool. Choosing the verb that matches the control keeps the test close to a person’s behavior.

A picture to hold

A hand. click is a press. fill is replacing the contents of a labeled blank, the way you would clear a form field and write the answer. press("Enter") is hitting one key. You would not repaint a whole form by tapping one key at a time unless the field truly requires it.

A small example

Line by line

  1. await page.goto("https://example.com");Start on a page that contains the link.
  2. getByRole("link", { name: "Learn more" }).click()Find the link and click it. click returns a promise. await waits until the click has been issued and the navigation it caused has settled.
  3. await expect(page).toHaveURL(/iana\.org/);The click should have changed the address. Assert the result, not only the fact that click returned.

Try it yourself

Type a note, press Save, and read the line the page shows.

Demo. fill replaces what was there. It does not add to the end.

Common mistakes

  • Using pressSequentially for every input. It is for fields that listen to each key. fill is the default for normal inputs.
  • Clicking a locator that matches two buttons. Playwright will complain that it resolved to more than one element. Make the name more specific.
  • Ignoring the promise. locator.click() without await lets the test end while the click is still happening.

Recap

Call the verb on a locator: click, fill, check, press, selectOption. await it. Then assert what changed.
