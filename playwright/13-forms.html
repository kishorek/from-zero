Forms

One idea. Find a field by its label, set it, then read what the page shows.

Start with a normal day

Asha fills in a form on a website. She writes her name beside Your name, ticks Subscribe, and presses Save.

The page answers with the words Saved Asha.

New words

import, test, expect, await, page, and getByRole mean the same as before.

setContent
Replace the tab with a small form written inside the check. Backticks wrap that form because it covers several lines.
label
The words tied to a field, such as Your name or Subscribe. getByLabel finds that field.
fill
Replace the field’s text with a new value. Here the value is Asha.
check
Tick a box and leave it ticked.
click
Press the Save button. Inside the page, addEventListener runs the page’s own steps on that press and writes Saved plus the name.
toHaveValue
Keep checking until the field holds this text. toBeChecked waits until the box is ticked. getByText finds words a person can read, and toBeVisible waits until those words are on the screen.

The steps, before any program

  1. Find the name field by the words Your name.
  2. Write Asha, and tick Subscribe.
  3. Press Save.
  4. Read the name, the tick, and the words Saved Asha.

The program only writes those steps down.

The same steps, written as a program

Read the steps once more. The box below is those same steps. The fields are found by the words a person can see.

What each line is saying

  1. <label>Your name <input name="name" /></label>The words Your name wrap the box. That wrap ties the label to the field.
  2. await page.getByLabel("Your name").fill("Asha");Find the field by its label and write Asha. fill replaces the text that was in the box.
  3. await page.getByLabel("Subscribe").check();Tick the Subscribe box and leave it ticked. The label is how the line finds it.
  4. await page.getByRole("button", { name: "Save" }).click();Press the Save button. The page’s own script then writes a result line.
  5. await expect(page.getByLabel("Your name")).toHaveValue("Asha");Read the field back. It should hold Asha.
  6. await expect(page.getByLabel("Subscribe")).toBeChecked();Check that Subscribe is ticked.
  7. await expect(page.getByText("Saved Asha")).toBeVisible();Check that a person can see the words Saved Asha. The check keeps looking until those words show.

Try it

Type a name, choose Milk, and press Save. The line under the button shows what you entered.

Practice. Use the labels. Type a name, choose a drink, and press Save.

Easy to mix up

  • The label is the way a person finds the field. The check uses that same label.
  • check leaves the box ticked. A click on the box switches it, so a second click would clear it.
  • After you fill a field, read the value back. The last lines of the test do that.

Remember

Find fields by their labels. fill and check set them, and the last lines read the value, the tick, and the words on the page.
