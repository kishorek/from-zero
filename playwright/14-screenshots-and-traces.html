Screenshots, video, and traces

One idea. Keep a picture when a test fails, so you can look instead of guess.

The idea

page.screenshot({ path: "page.png", fullPage: true }) writes a picture. In the runner you rarely call that yourself. Set screenshot: "only-on-failure" and trace: "on-first-retry" in the config. A trace is a zip of actions, DOM snapshots, console lines, and network calls.

Open a trace with npx playwright show-trace trace.zip, or open the HTML report with npx playwright show-report after a run. The report links the trace, the screenshot, and the error.

Why it matters

A failed expectation tells you the text was wrong. A trace tells you what the page looked like and which step got you there. That is the difference between guessing and looking.

A picture to hold

A shop camera. You do not watch the tape after a quiet day. You watch it when something went missing. only-on-failure and on-first-retry are the policy “record when there is a reason.” Recording every success fills the disk and teaches you little.

A small example

Retries belong in configuration: retries: 1 while you learn, and often retries: 2 only in CI. The trace setting on-first-retry records the second attempt, which is the interesting one. The configuration lesson places these lines in a whole file.

Line by line

  1. await page.screenshot({ path: "page.png", fullPage: true });fullPage captures below the fold. Without it you get the viewport, the part a window would show.
  2. screenshot: "only-on-failure"The runner takes the picture when a test fails. Passing tests stay quiet.
  3. trace: "on-first-retry"Record a trace if the test is running again after a failure. Open it from the HTML report.
  4. video: "retain-on-failure"Keep a video only when the test failed. Videos are larger than traces. Turn them on when motion is the bug.

Common mistakes

  • Turning video on for every test forever. The archive gets huge, and nobody watches the passes.
  • Storing screenshots of pages that contain real customer data and then committing them. They are evidence, and they can also be private.
  • Debugging from memory when a trace is sitting in the report. Open the trace before you change the test.

Recap

Screenshots, traces, and video explain failures. Prefer only-on-failure and on-first-retry. Open the HTML report and the trace before you rewrite the test.
